# StakeLane demo

Allow two and a half minutes for a recorded walkthrough. Use the checked-in reports for the measured results. A live one-round run demonstrates the command and yields its own transaction hashes. [Mumbai submission and video guidance](https://ethglobal.com/events/mumbai/info/details)

1. **0:00–0:25 — Show who waits.** Wallet A sends eight expensive, valid frame transactions. Wallet B sends one cheap transaction. Explain that the sidecar decides when it can start checking B. The block builder decides inclusion later.
2. **0:25–0:55 — Show the queue.** Open the [scheduler](sidecar/packages/executor/src/services/AdmissionScheduler/index.ts). FIFO dispatches jobs in arrival order across four slots. Fair mode rotates among sender addresses and allows at most two heavy admissions from a sender. Multiple users of one sending contract share its turn.
3. **0:55–1:35 — Show the turn order and cost.** Run `./stakelane report sidecar/evidence/compare/merged-main-7d052e7 --verify`. In round 0 of the eight-heavy Mac run, B’s simulation started after all eight A simulations finished under FIFO. Under fair scheduling, two had finished and six had yet to start. Across 20 rounds B’s p95 fell from 678 ms to 238 ms and A’s median batch took 29 ms longer. [FIFO trials](sidecar/evidence/compare/merged-main-7d052e7/fifo.json) · [Fair trials](sidecar/evidence/compare/merged-main-7d052e7/fair.json)
4. **1:35–2:05 — Show what is reproducible.** Run `./stakelane report sidecar/evidence/hardening/full-1 --verify` for Cresnex’s separate Linux comparison. Show the policy settings and a saved private receipt. The heavy workload used 993,512 VERIFY gas in every mode. Local receipts establish inclusion after admission timing.
5. **2:05–2:30 — Show the public evidence and limits.** Open the [public relay record](docs/PUBLIC_RELAY_REPORT.md). The organizers’ sidecar admitted our transaction on chain 1337 and later cancelled it after four relay attempts. The timing experiments ran on private chain 8141337. Explain that the workload is synthetic and that a sender can use multiple addresses.

To demonstrate the current checkout live, run `./stakelane doctor` and `./stakelane compare --rounds 1 --bursts 8` after the [one-time setup](docs/OPERATOR.md). Show whether B got a turn and the corresponding cost to A. Use the full archived runs for percentile claims.
